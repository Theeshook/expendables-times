The Window Is Shut

The summer transfer window closed on 1 September. Whatever your squad looks like now, the clubs are done buying — from here the only transfers that matter are the ones you make yourself, one free move a week.

What that changes for you. No more waiting on a signing to justify a hold. Price rises and falls are now driven purely by what managers do, and a player who was rotated out of a squad in August is not getting rescued by a deadline-day arrival. The wire below has the fallout.

What Changed in the Rules

the six changes for 2026/27 — tap to open
  1. 1. Live points, live tables. Points now update while matches are being played — appearances, goals, assists, clean sheets and defensive contribution. Mini-league standings move in real time too, so the Expendables table will be live all afternoon. New
  2. 2. Projected bonus after 20 minutes. Provisional bonus appears once a match passes the 20-minute mark, and shifts as the game goes on. It is a projection, not a result — it can and will change. New
  3. 3. Bonus Points System reworked. Rebuilt to cut the overlap where one player earned both BPS and defensive contribution points for the same actions. Goalkeepers, full-backs and attackers are all expected to see more bonus. Changed
  4. 4. Price prediction tool. FPL now shows you who is likely to rise or fall, tracking transfer activity against the daily price change at midnight UK time. New
  5. 5. Scores finalise later. Gameweek lockdown moves to 09:00 UK time the morning after the last match, rather than an hour after the final whistle — extra time for Opta to review the stats. Changed
  6. 6. Chips are unchanged. Two full sets again — Wildcard, Free Hit, Triple Captain and Bench Boost, one of each per half of the season. Same
What this means for our money. Later lockdown at 09:00 UK is 4:00am ET — so Sunday-night scores are not final until Monday morning. The Times goes out Tuesday for exactly this reason, and no block or weekly-high prize is settled on provisional bonus.
